# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

📊 **[Click here to download the Interactive Power BI Dashboard (.pbix)](customer_behavior_dashboard.pbix)**

## Project Overview
This project analyzes customer shopping behavior using transactional data from 3,900 purchases across various product categories to uncover insights into spending patterns, customer segments, and subscription behavior[cite: 4]. The goal is to guide strategic business decisions and optimize marketing strategies[cite: 3].

## Tech Stack
* **Data Cleaning & Exploration:** Python (Pandas)[cite: 4]
* **Data Analysis & Modeling:** SQL (PostgreSQL)[cite: 4]
* **Data Visualization:** Power BI[cite: 3]

## Key Insights
* **Demographics:** Female customers generate a slightly higher total revenue (53%) compared to male customers[cite: 4].
* **Subscriptions:** Subscribers spend 68% more than non-subscribers and account for 78% of the total revenue[cite: 2].
* **Shipping:** Express shipping customers spend 12% more per transaction ($65 average) compared to standard shipping ($58 average)[cite: 2].
* **Top Products:** Blouses and Dresses received the highest customer satisfaction (5-star ratings)[cite: 2].

## Strategic Recommendations
1. **Boost Subscriptions:** Promote exclusive, high-value benefits specifically designed for active subscribers[cite: 2].
2. **Loyalty Programs:** Reward repeat buyers systematically to increase long-term customer retention and move them into the "Loyal" segment[cite: 2, 4].
3. **Targeted Marketing:** Focus marketing spend heavily on high-revenue segments and express shipping users[cite: 2].
4. **Product Positioning:** Highlight top-rated products prominently in all major acquisition campaigns[cite: 2].
